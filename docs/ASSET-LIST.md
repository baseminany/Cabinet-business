- `product-reading-nook.jpg` — 60–72 inch bench with lower drawers/cubbies and simple shelves or book ledges above.

- `product-kids-bed-storage.jpg` — low twin storage bed / floor bed with drawers or cubbies. Keep it simple; no bunk-bed claims until compliance work is done.

Legacy slots that should be phased out or remapped:
- `project-pantry-hero.jpg`, `space-pantry.jpg`, `space-small-kitchen.jpg`, `space-butler-pantry.jpg`, `project-kitchen-feature.jpg` were from the earlier full-custom cabinetry direction. Do not lead with these unless the strategy shifts back.

Image direction:
- Believable smaller products first. The images should look like real models a small garage shop could build, crate, and ship.
- Avoid huge walk-in pantries, full kitchens, luxury mansions, or giant built-ins that imply a full install crew.
- Avoid repeating one room with different colors.
- Use realistic normal homes, warm natural light, walnut/oak/rift-oak accents, smooth painted finishes, restrained brass or black hardware, and slightly imperfect lived-in styling.
- No people, logos, text, watermarks, fake brand marks, or obviously AI-perfect symmetry.
