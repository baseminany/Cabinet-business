# Product safety gate

This is an operating checklist, not legal advice. Children’s products remain visible for demand discovery, but `launchDecision()` blocks payment until the product-specific file is signed off. Small-batch production is not a blanket exemption from children’s-product obligations.

## Required file for every children’s SKU

1. Intended age and foreseeable use/misuse written down.
2. Applicable federal and state rules identified with a qualified lab/compliance adviser.
3. Material/finish supplier records, test plan, required third-party reports, Children’s Product Certificate, and tracking-label design complete where applicable.
4. Physical first article checked for sharp edges, protrusions, small parts, pinch/shear points, entrapment openings, tip-over, wall anchoring, and instruction clarity.
5. People-load products additionally tested for static/dynamic load, stability, climbing/misuse, wear, and fall hazards using a written protocol and retained results.
6. Final BOM, revision, supplier lots, photos, warnings, age grading, instructions, pack-out, and approval date retained together.
7. Complaint/incident log and stop-sale/recall procedure ready before the first order.

## Recommended launch order

1. Montessori forward-facing bookshelf: highest demand hypothesis; anti-tip system and child-product file required.
2. Book ledges: simple geometry; mounting instructions and fastener/wall assumptions required.
3. Low open toy storage only after stability, edge, and entrapment review.
4. Learning tower and step stool last. They are people-load/fall products and need a materially stronger test and review program.

Do not use “safe,” “non-toxic,” an unsupported load rating, or an age claim in public copy until the supporting file exists. Do not open checkout merely because a prototype feels sturdy.
