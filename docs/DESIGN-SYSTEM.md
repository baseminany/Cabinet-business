# Design System — House of Nook

## North star
House of Nook is a warm, premium, family-home brand for shippable modular built-ins. It should feel refined and editorial, but not like a full-service luxury mansion cabinetry firm. The product promise is smaller, useful, beautiful systems that can realistically be built in modules and shipped: mudroom nooks, coffee nooks, laundry nooks, Montessori/playroom storage, reading benches, and simple storage beds.

## Positioning rules
- Lead with **nooks / systems / modules**, not full kitchens or whole-home cabinetry.
- Every visual and copy decision should feel garage-buildable and shippable.
- Avoid implying full-room remodels, giant walk-in pantries, or luxury estate-scale projects.
- Bunk beds and safety-sensitive children products are future-only until compliance review; low storage beds are acceptable as planning inspiration.

## Palette
- Warm white `#fffdf8` — primary page background and customer-facing panels.
- Parchment `#ece1cf` — soft section contrast and selected card surface.
- Porcelain `#f7f3ea` — light cards and planner panels.
- Champagne `#d9c6a3` — hairlines, dividers, glow, and soft luxury edge.
- Brass `#b88a44` — selected states, progress, icon accent.
- Walnut `#4b2e20` — main CTA, warm contrast, wood cue.
- Espresso `#211813` — deep shadows and premium dark moments.
- Deep green `#173b33` — subtle status badges.
- Ink `#14110e`, soft ink `#45403a`, muted ink `#8a847c` — text hierarchy.

## Planner / post-landing UI
The planner must visually follow the landing page: warm ivory surfaces, parchment/champagne borders, walnut CTAs, brass selected states, and minimal black. Avoid a heavy black app shell after the landing page.

## 3D palette
- Scene background: warm ivory/parchment, not gray or black.
- Walls: warm plaster `#eadfce`, with subtle side tint `#d9c8ad` and readable corner markers.
- Floor: warm oak `#b67a45`, with darker plank lines `#7c4f2c` and a shadow line where wall meets floor.
- Trim: warm white `#fff8ed`.
- Windows/glass: blue-gray `#9bb8c2` with visible casing.
- Selected modules: brass/walnut outlines, never neon.
- Modules need door reveals, pulls, depth cues, and separated materials so the room does not become beige-on-beige.

## Typography
- Use Inter for most UI, controls, labels, buttons, and numbers.
- Large headlines should be warm, slightly editorial, and less aggressive than the earlier heavy-black Studio direction.
- Eyebrows stay small uppercase with wide tracking.

## Components
- Primary buttons: walnut/espresso with warm-white text.
- Secondary buttons: warm-white with champagne/brass border.
- Cards: warm-white/porcelain with champagne hairline and layered soft shadows.
- Controls: rounded but not childish; generous touch targets; advanced controls hidden until requested.

## What to avoid
- Beige-on-beige 3D scenes where walls, floor, and modules blend together.
- Heavy black planner shells that do not match the landing page.
- Giant fantasy cabinetry imagery that looks unshippable.
- Generic SaaS gradients or bright blue accents.
- Fake completion language for AI/photo features.
